# The Fledge issue format

This is the format of the issues in `Fledge-Software/Fledge-Workspace`, taken
from the ones that worked best (the Phase 8 epic, #79; the MCP resources
task, #86; the decision issues #323 and #324). Follow the target
repository's own `CLAUDE.md` / `AGENTS.md` and issue templates where they
differ.

## The bar

From AGENTS.md: **issues are written to educate.** Assume the reader has no
context: a new contributor, a future maintainer, or the same person six
months later. Someone who has never seen the plan should understand both the
task and why it matters.

- **Say why before what.**
- **Define the terms and name the files.** Real paths, tables, routes,
  migrations and issue numbers, so the reader can go and look. (This is the
  opposite of `to-tickets`' "no file paths" rule, and it wins.)
- **State what breaks if it is skipped**, and what "done" looks like.
- **Show the reasoning, including what was rejected.**
- **Write prose, not fragments.** No bare ticket references: "#272 split this
  out because…", never just "see #272".
- **Quote the source when it matters**, such as a decision's exact words, and
  link it.
- **No claim without a source.** An assumption is marked ⚠️.

Every issue ends with the attribution footer:

```

---
_Generated by [Claude Code](https://claude.ai/code)_
```

## Structure on the tracker

| Plan | Tracker |
|---|---|
| a phase | a **milestone**, `Phase <N> — <Name>`, and an **epic** issue in it |
| a piece of work | an **issue** in the milestone, attached to the epic as a sub-issue |
| a decision that can wait | an issue labelled **`decision`** |
| a dependency | "Blocked by #n" in the body, plus a native blocking link where there is one |

**Labels.** Use only labels the repository already has, and check them
first (`gh label list`, or `get_label` per name). Fledge-Workspace has
`epic`, `decision` ("needs an owner's call before the work can start"),
`bug`, priorities `P0` ("blocks everything else, or loses data/money") to
`P3` ("hygiene: cheap, low risk, easy to lose"), and sizes. It has **no**
`ready` or `design` label, although `/issuecoder` and `/designflow` look for
them. A new label needs the user's approval in the breakdown.

**Size**, as Fledge-Workspace's labels define it: `size:S` is half a day;
`size:M` is a day or two; `size:L` is a week; `size:XL` is multi-week and
should probably be an epic. An issue should fit one `/issuecoder` run, so
most should be S or M, and an XL is a sign to split it.

**Titles.**
- Epic: `Epic: Phase <N> — <Name>`.
- Work: `<Area> <n> — <what it does, in plain words>`, e.g. `MCP 7 — Expose
  docs and tables as MCP resources with stable URIs`. Once the area has no
  numbering scheme, use `<Area>: <what it does>`.
- Decision: says what needs deciding, e.g. `MCP resource subscriptions need a
  hosting decision before they need code`.

## Epic

```markdown
**Status: <one line — what exists today, and where this scope came from>.**

## What it is

<The thing, defined for someone who has never heard of it. Why it matters
to the product.>

## Why it comes where it does

<Its place in the sequence, what it waits for, and anything that can ship
earlier.>

## Shape

<The decisions that shape the whole phase, each with the alternative that
lost. A table where it helps.>

## Guardrails

<Rules every issue under this epic must respect, and why.>

## Done when

<The phase's exit criteria, from the plan.>

Plan: `<path to the plan>` §<section>.
```

## Work issue

```markdown
**<A bold lede of one or two sentences: the concept, defined, and why it
matters.>**

## Why this matters

<The reason the work exists and who it is for. What goes wrong, and for
whom, if it is skipped.>

## What exists today

<The relevant files, tables and routes, with paths. What is half-built.>

## Work

- [ ] <a checkable piece of work, with the reason when it isn't obvious>
- [ ] <…>

## What "done" looks like

<Observable, checkable outcomes. Tests that must exist.>

## Watch

<Traps: rules this work must not break, things that look like they should be
changed but must not be, and anything a reader would trip over. Omit if
there are none.>

Blocked by: #<n> (<why>), or "nothing, can start now".
Parent: #<epic>. Plan: `<path>` §<section>.
```

## Decision issue

Label: `decision`. Its "done" is a recorded decision, not code.

```markdown
## Why this needs a decision

<What the question is, why it can't be settled in code, and what it blocks.>

## What we know

<The facts, with sources. What is already decided nearby, quoted.>

## Options

1. **<Option>.** <What it means, what it costs, what it gives up.>
2. **<Option>.** <…>

<A recommendation, if there is one, and why.>

## What "done" looks like

Not code: a decision, recorded here, on <the question>. Until then,
<what must stay as it is>.

Blocks: #<n>. Parent: #<epic>. Plan: `<path>` §<section>.
```
